Multimodal extraction

Upload a proposal PDF. Its embedded text is pulled out directly (no OCR, no layout analysis - that step alone measured 5+ minutes on a real document and made this page too slow to be useful). Only the pages likely to contain a table, chart or other visual content are also rendered as images and sent alongside the text - detected cheaply, without the model, by checking each page for embedded pictures or a high density of drawn lines/shapes (table grids, chart axes). A document with no such pages gets one fast text-only call; images only scale with how much of the document is actually visual, not with page count. Pages that do need images are batched automatically behind the scenes (the same way, and for the same request-size reasons, as the images-only path elsewhere in this project) - there is nothing to configure here. Same downstream review, approval and save steps as the Markdown-only extraction page.
