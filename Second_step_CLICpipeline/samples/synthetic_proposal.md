# H2-RURAL: Sustainable Hydrogen Storage for Rural Microgrids

**Acronym:** H2-RURAL
**Call identifier:** HORIZON-CL5-2026-D3-01
**Submission date:** 15 March 2026
**Proposal status:** Pending

## 1. Summary

H2-RURAL develops a modular hydrogen storage unit that lets remote villages keep renewable energy produced in summer
for use in winter. This proposal builds on the results of the H2-GRID project funded under Horizon Europe, whose
electrolyser prototype is reused here at larger scale.

## 3. Demonstration activities

The project runs three demonstration activities:

| Code | Activity | Site |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Pilot microgrid with 200 kW storage | Kalmar, Sweden |
| D2 | Field test of the cold-climate electrolyser | Aragon, Spain |
| D3 | Community operation trial | Atacama, Chile |

## 5. Impact and Sustainable Development Goals

The project contributes to SDG 7, 9 and 13 by making clean energy storage affordable in regions without grid access,
by strengthening local industry, and by cutting diesel use in remote communities.
