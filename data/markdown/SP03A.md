## Letter of support for the project RESENSE

Dear colleagues, We are pleased to confirm our interest in the proposed project and our intention to take part in the consortium. The topic corresponds closely to our own development priorities and to the needs we meet in daily contact with customers. Our company has experience in the design and production of measurement equipment and in supporting customers during installation and operation. We are ready to share this experience with the research partners during the project. We will make test equipment and technical expertise available for the work, and our specialists will take part in planning and evaluation meetings. This support is described below and is given in addition to our own funded participation.

2 February 2027

Inkindsupport Confirmationbythecompany

We confirm in kind support in the form of test equipment and specialist working time, with an estimated total value of EUR 24 000, to be provided during the project period.

We regard the results as relevant for future products and services, and we intend to follow the work closely. We would welcome the opportunity to host demonstrations at our own facilities if the schedule permits.

We wish the consortium every success and look forward to a productive collaboration. Please do not hesitate to contact us if further information is needed.

Our commitment is conditional on the project receiving funding and on the conclusion of a consortium agreement that is acceptable to all parties. We confirm that this letter reflects the position of our organisation at the time of writing.

Yours sincerely,

Authorised signatory Vantora Sensor Solutions Ltd

## C.4 Methodology

Ethical and legal aspects are considered from the beginning. Measurement data from partner sites is handled under written agreements, and any information that could identify individuals is excluded from collection.

Analysis combines statistical methods with model based reasoning. The team compares alternative approaches on common test material so that differences can be attributed to the methods rather than to the data.

Interviews and workshops with future users complement the technical work. Their purpose is to understand how monitoring information is used in practice and which presentation of results supports good decisions.
